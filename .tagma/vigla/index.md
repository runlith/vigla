Vigla is an experimental fork of the whole Svelte stack — compiler, runtime, tooling and SvelteKit — rewritten in TypeScript under runlith: compatible with existing Svelte 5 runes code, native in TSX with runes. The core, the Svelte adapter, the Vite plugin, Kit and its adapters are built and published, and Tagma runs on them; the ideas below are directions not yet built.
