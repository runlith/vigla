Dromon is an experimental fork of the whole Svelte stack — compiler, runtime, tooling and SvelteKit — rewritten in TypeScript under runlith: compatible with existing Svelte 5 runes code, native in TSX with runes. Nothing is built yet; the ideas below are the directions agreed so far, not specs.
