A dromon (δρόμων, "runner") was the Byzantine war galley, the fleet's fastest ship: speed is the framework's promise, and the name stands in Tagma's family — a tagma was a Byzantine guard unit.

**No "svelte" in the name.** Svelte's [branding guidelines](https://github.com/sveltejs/branding) cover its logo and ask that use imply no endorsement and that compatibility claims be accurate; no Svelte rule on fork names was found. Common FOSS practice renames forks (Valkey, OpenTofu, Forgejo, MariaDB), and a `svelte-*` name reads as one more plugin among hundreds. Compatibility rides the tagline and the tests; drop-in works by a package-manager alias whatever the name.

**Rejected.** Syntagma — long, a linguistics term, `syntagma.dev` taken. Tessera — `tessera`, `tesserae` and `tessella` taken on npm. Praetor — misspelled easily, `praetor` taken on npm and `praetor.dev` registered, and a judge's name fits a rules product better than a UI framework.

**Pronunciation.** Russian dictionaries stress the first syllable, дро́мон [ˈdromən]; Russian speakers will likely say дромо́н by analogy with вагон and патрон (inference). English reads it with the stress on the first syllable (not checked against a dictionary).

**Availability on 2026-10-29.** npm `dromon` was free. `dromon.dev` is registered (RDAP: since 2026-03-19, expires 2027-03-19) with no DNS records; `dromon.com` is registered since 2001. No registration was found (whois or RDAP) for `dromonjs.dev`, `dromonjs.com`, `dromonjs.org`, `dromon.io`, `dromon.org`, `dromon.sh`, `dromon.build`, `getdromon.dev`, `usedromon.dev`; no registrar was asked.

**Extension.** `.dro` was picked over `.dmn` (OMG's Decision Model and Notation, opened by Camunda) and `.drm` (read as DRM; several unrelated formats) — all three free in GitHub linguist. The two-input decision made it unnecessary: own components are `.tsx`, compatibility is `.svelte`.
